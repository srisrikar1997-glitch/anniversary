Happy Anniversary, Ramani

The best moments of my life got unlocked with your subscription.

It all got started with that candlelit date we had, and since then, life has taken us through mountains and valleys, beaches and oceans. We have seen our share of ups and downs, and through all of it, we have built moments worth savoring to remember the very best of us.

For the longest time, I haven't known how to put my feelings into words. Whenever you ask me why I love you, my mind goes blank and I end up staying quiet. I always thought what I felt for you was just blind love—something I felt deeply but couldn't explain. Because I could never find the right words, I never properly took the opportunity to tell you why I love you so much.

I also know that you are a deeply romantic person who loves surprises, and you ended up with a guy who isn't naturally good at grand gestures. Yet, you stayed patient with me. When I actually sit down and look at our life together, my love for you isn't blind at all. I have so many reasons why it has always been you.

I love you for the warmth you bring into my quiet world. I love the feeling I get when you let your guard down and behave cute with me. I love when you surprise me by cooking a special dish just to make me happy. I might not do big, flashy surprises, but taking you out after a long, exhausting day is my favorite way to unwind—because being around you doesn't drain my energy, it brings me back to life. And at the end of every day, simply getting to hug you and fall asleep is the only place I feel completely at peace.

I love you for how much you have grown with me. We used to fight a lot, and things used to get heated so quickly. Now, I see how much effort you put into calming down and explaining things to me gently instead of fighting. Watching you soften your anger just so we can understand each other makes me respect you more than I usually say out loud, and it makes me never, ever want to hurt you.

Most of all, I love you because you are 100% on my team. In every situation, you make me feel like your first priority. I love the way you stand up for me and look out for my happiness. Knowing that you always have my back makes me feel cared for in a way nobody else ever has.

I miss you the second you aren't around because you are my comfort, my peace, and my home. I may not always be the most emotional or expressive guy in the room, but every single day, I am grateful that my life is tied to yours.

Happy Anniversary, Ramani. Here is to renewing this subscription for the rest of our lives.

Forever yours,Srikar
